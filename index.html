Good to know

Common questions.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Call or text with a few details and photos if you can, and we'll go over the job and give you a clear price before any work starts.

Do you work with insurance claims?

For storm, fire and water damage, we can document the damage with photos and provide a written estimate to support your claim. Call to talk through your situation.

How fast can you come out?

Call or text and we'll get back to you quickly. For urgent damage like active leaks, tell us right away so we can prioritize it.

What areas do you serve?

McComb and the surrounding area. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

What kinds of jobs do you take?

Property damage restoration, furniture and auto restoration, plumbing, TV mounting, landscaping, and cemetery and monument restoration.
